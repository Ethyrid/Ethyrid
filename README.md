### About Me

I build games where **story, mechanics, and levels trust the player's intelligence**—designing rules and spatial layouts, implementing them in **Unity (C#)**, and stress-testing edge cases before scaling art.

- **Intern @ [El Zorro Azul](https://ethyrid.dev/#contacto)** *(Apr 2026 – Present)* — 3D *game-ready* modeling in **Blender** (optimized topology) and full asset integration pipeline into **Unity**.
- **Universidad de Mendoza** *(2024 – Est. Feb 2027)* — *Technical Degree in Game Development (Programming Specialization)*. Prior coursework in Law & Industrial Design at UNCuyo.
- **Systems & Modding** — Conflict auditing, SSEEdit/Creation Kit patching, and Papyrus scripting across **1,380 mods** in *Skyrim SE*, *Minecraft Fabric* (313 mods), *Valheim*, and *Project Zomboid*.
